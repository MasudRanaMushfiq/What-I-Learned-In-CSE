

# Digital Signal Processing Lab

### Course Information
**Course:** CSE 3222 (Digital Signal Processing Lab)
**Course Type:** Laboratory Work, 1 Credit
**Prerequisite:** MATH 2241: Linear Algebra

### Instructor
Dr. Md. Ekramul Hamid, Professor, Dept. of CSE, University of Rajshahi 

---

### Course Motivation
> To know basic of the digital signal processing concepts and its analysis.

---

## Lab Experiments

| #   | Experiment                                                                                   |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Develop programs for generating Sine, Cosine, Unit Step, Unit ramp, and exponential signals. |
| 2   | Finding convolution, Autocorrelation, and cross correlation of sequences.                    |
| 3   | Develop programs for DFT, z-transform, and inverse z-transform.                              |
| 4   | Design FIR and IIR Filters.                                                                  |
| 5   | Show sampling effects (aliasing).                                                            |
| 6   | Show filtering effects (low pass, windowing) and signal reconstruction.                      |
| 7   | Add noise to speech, show FFT changes, and use adaptive filters on noisy speech.             |

# Operating Systems Lab

### Course Information
**Course:** CSE 3242 (Operating Systems Lab)
**Course Type:** Laboratory Work, 1 Credit
**Prerequisite:** CSE1111 Introduction to Computer Systems, CSE2121 Data Structure, CSE2231 Computer Architecture and Organization

### Instructor
Mr. Kazi Jahidur Rahman, Assistant Professor, Dept. of CSE, University of Rajshahi 

---

### Course Motivation
> To develop basics on Operating System Design and to analyze its different features.

---

## Lab Experiments

| #   | Experiment                                                                    |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Basics of UNIX/LINUX commands and shell programming.                          |
| 2   | Implementation of CPU scheduling (RR, SJF, FCFS, Priority).                   |
| 3   | Implement file allocation strategies, and Bankers algorithm.                  |
| 4   | Implement page replacement algorithms (FIFO, LRU, LFU) and paging techniques. |
