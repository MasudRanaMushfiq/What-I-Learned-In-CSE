

## Undergraduate CSE Course Structure | University of Rajshahi(2021-22)

---
- A complete list of courses offered in the **B.Sc. Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering** program at the **University of Rajshahi**, session **2021–2022**. 
- The program spans **four academic years** across eight semesters, totaling **160 credits**. Courses range from foundational sciences and mathematics in the early years to advanced topics like **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cryptography** in the final year. 
- Students also complete a **thesis or project** in the fourth year, with elective options in areas such as **Deep Learning, Digital Image Processing, and Blockchain**. 
- Each semester pairs theory courses with hands-on lab work to build both conceptual and practical skills.
