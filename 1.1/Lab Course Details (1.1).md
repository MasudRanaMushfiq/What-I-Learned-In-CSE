

# Basic Electronics Lab

### Course Information
**Course:** EEE 1132 (Basic Electronics Lab)
**Course Type:** Laboratory Work, 1 Credit
**Prerequisite:** None

### Instructor
Dr. Bimal Kumar Pramanik, Professor, Dept. of CSE, University of Rajshahi

---

### Course Motivation
> To develop basic knowledge on Electrical circuits and Electronics.

## Lab Experiments

| #   | Experiment                                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Study I-V characteristics of a P-N junction diode                                                                   |
| 2   | Use PN junction diode as half-wave and full-wave rectifier                                                          |
| 3   | Study I-V characteristics of Zener diode and use it as a voltage regulator by varying $V_I$ and $R_L$               |
| 5   | Study the characteristics of Op-Amp in inverting and non-inverting mode and use it as an adder                      |

# Computer Maintenance and Engineering Drawing Lab

### Course Information
**Course:** Computer Maintenance and Engineering Drawing Lab
**Course Type:** Laboratory Work, 1 Credit
**Prerequisite:** None

### Instructor
Md. Hafizur Rahman Joarder(Sata/Pata), Senior programmer, Officer,  Dept. of CSE, University of Rajshahi


### Course Motivation
> To study how to assemble and disassemble a computer and install different types of operating systems.

---

## Lab Experiments

### Computer Maintenance

| #   | Experiment                                                                        |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Assemble different parts of a computer (RAM, SSD, HDD)                            |
| 2   | Partition a computer hard disk while giving OS                                    |
| 3   | Install different types of operating systems (Windows, Linux, etc.) and Duel Boot |
| 4   | Install different types of application software and utility software              |
| 5   | Fault finding — detect hardware related problems in PC and find the solution      |
| 6   | Get familiar with DOS and its commands                                            |
